I woke up by myself.\
And then I felt the room was hotter than a sauna.\
And then I felt I needed to pee.\
And then I opened my eyes.\
And then the room was neither light nor dark.\
And then I reached for my phone.\
And then I saw no new messages since last night.\
And then I did today's morning meditation with the [Balance](https://www.balanceapp.com/referral-social) app.\
And then the voice of Leah told me to write down something I love about a person in my life.\
And then I wrote about my neighbor Marija, who I perceive as kind and generous without expecting something in return.\
And then the meditation was finished.\
And then I saw the streak of 21 days.\
And then I said to myself "wee hoo you are getting there champ. One day at a time".\
And then I decided it was going to be yet another Lazy Sunday.\
And then I took a couple of exactly three or so deep breaths.\
And then I closed my eyes again.\
And then I avoided the chores and responsibilities lurking around the room.\
And then I slept like a man baby.\
And then I dreamt everything was fine.\
And then I was happily at peace.

Rasmus. On the morning of Sunday, November 8, 2026, after 9:30.

Inspiration from this came from the Swedish TV-show [Agent-ish](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt44113326/) where they use a similar writing style to depict the biography of Truls Möregårdh.
